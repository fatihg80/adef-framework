# Product Vision

## Purpose
Define the problem worth solving and the long-term direction of the product.

## Vision statement
`For [target users], who [need/problem], [product] is a [category] that [primary value]. Unlike [alternative], it [key differentiation].`

## Problem
- What problem exists?
- Who experiences it?
- What is the cost of leaving it unsolved?

## Desired outcomes
- Business outcomes
- User outcomes
- Engineering outcomes

## Principles
List durable product principles that guide trade-offs.

## Non-goals
List what the product is explicitly not trying to become.
